Privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026

In short
  • No account, no sign-up.
  • Your progress stays on your phone.
  • Ads are non-personalised. Remove Ads switches off the ads between levels.
  • We never sell your data.

Mandala Pop is made by Adrien Buffin, an independent developer on the Gold Coast, Australia (“we”). This policy covers the Mandala Pop app on iPhone and Android.

What stays on your phone

Your levels, coins, gems, pals, garden and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Weekly Cup

To run the online Weekly Cup leaderboard, the game creates an anonymous ID for your device through Google Firebase and stores your random player nickname (for example “Yuki42”) and your cup points. It is not linked to your name, email or identity.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and pays for itself with ads served by Google AdMob. You can choose to watch short videos for rewards, such as continuing a level or doubling your coins, and from level 8 a short ad may appear between some levels. Buying Remove Ads switches off the ads between levels for good.

We ask Google for non-personalised ads only, so ads are not chosen from your interests or your activity in other apps, and the game never asks to track you. To show and count ads and to prevent fraud, Google may still process your device's advertising identifier, IP address (which gives a rough location such as your country), device type and how you interact with the ad. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Game analytics (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to see how the game is played so we can make it better: for example which levels people start and finish, how long sessions last, and whether players come back. This uses a random app-instance ID plus device model, operating-system version, language and country. It is not linked to your name or email and is not used for advertising. See Firebase privacy.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We only receive confirmation that a purchase happened, never your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your phone, such as a free daily spin or an egg about to hatch. They are created on the device, not sent from a server, and you can turn them off in your phone's settings.

We never sell your data

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services named on this page, which process it for us.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Reset your advertising ID: on iPhone, Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; on Android, Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Deleting the game removes everything stored on your device.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email us at the address below.

There's no sound

Check that Sound effects and Music are on in Options (gear icon on the home screen), and turn the volume up. On iPhone, update to the latest version of the game: older versions stayed silent when the phone was on silent mode.

How do I remove ads?

Options › Remove ads. It's a one-time purchase. Already bought it on this account? Tap Restore in the same place.

My progress disappeared

Progress lives only on your phone. It is lost if the app is deleted or the phone is reset without a backup.

How do pals work?

Win levels to earn treats, feed your pal, and it grows from Baby to Grown to Legendary. Each pal gives a power at the start of a level. Eggs hatch new pals.
